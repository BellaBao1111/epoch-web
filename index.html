The creed

Most strategy on a phone is a timer wearing armor. Epoch keeps what makes the genre worth playing (scouting, timing, terrain, the gamble of an early attack) and removes the clicking that never fit a touchscreen.

Questions

Asked before marching

What is Epoch?

A real-time strategy game built for the iPhone and played in landscape. You explore a fogged map, grow an economy, capture villages and destroy the opposing headquarters. There are no overnight timers and nothing to collect while you are away.

Can I play it now?

Not publicly. Epoch is a playable prototype in closed beta: some art is still procedural, the AI is simple, and balance and performance are being tested on real phones. Access is by invitation while that work continues.

How long is a match?

The game is designed around ten to fifteen minutes: long enough for an economy, a scouting mistake and a comeback, short enough for one sitting.

How do you command an army with one thumb?

Hold, drag, release. Hold an army's banner, drag to a place or a target, and let go. Building and training work the same way, and dragging back to where you started cancels. Orders persist, so you set intent instead of babysitting.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. A match seats two to four players, human or AI, in whatever teams you set. Today that means invited friend rooms; public matchmaking and accounts are not built yet.

Which devices?

iPhone first, in landscape. Mac builds exist for development and testing. iPad, Android and PC are possibilities, not promises.
